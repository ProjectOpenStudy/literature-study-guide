Q3. Through which poet is the main line of Rossetti's influence and romanticism traced? A. Algernon Charles Swinburne B. Matthew Arnold C. William Morris D. James Thomson Answer: C
Q4. Which of the following is an early work by William Morris? A. The Earthly Paradise B. Atalanta in Calydon C. The White Ship D. Sister Helen Answer: A
Q5. Under the influence of Ruskin and a revulsion from modern commercialism, what political ideology did William Morris adopt? A. Conservatism B. Socialism C. Anarchism D. Imperialism Answer: B
Q6. How did Morris once describe himself early in his career? A. A scorpion B. The idle singer of an empty day C. The poetic exponent of liberalism D. A wandering minstrel Answer: B
Q7. Which poet was the last to die of the great race of Victorian poets? A. Robert Browning B. Lord Tennyson C. Algernon Charles Swinburne D. William Morris Answer: C
Q8. Swinburne's 'Atalanta in Calydon' and 'Erechtheus' rank among the finest attempts to revive which form? A. The medieval mystery play B. The English sonnet sequence C. The pure classic form of Greek tragedy D. The Italian epic Answer: C
Q9. Swinburne wrote an English dramatic trilogy about which historical figure? A. Mary Stuart B. Queen Elizabeth C. Joan of Arc D. Cleopatra Answer: A
