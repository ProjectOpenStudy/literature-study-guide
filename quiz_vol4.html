▶ Page 229 - Swinburne and Minor Poets

Q1. It is primarily as what type of poet that Swinburne will hold his place in our literature? A. An epic poet B. A dramatist C. A lyrist D. A satirist Answer: C
Q2. What was a frequent snare for Swinburne in his poetry? A. His excessive political conservatism B. His fondness for mere verbal effects C. His inability to write in meter D. His strict adherence to realism Answer: B
Q3. Which of the following works is a romantic comedy by Sir Henry Taylor? A. Philip van Artevelde B. The Virgin Widow, or A Sicilian Summer C. The Quest of the Sangraal D. Proverbial Philosophy Answer: B
Q4. Robert Stephen Hawker was the extremely eccentric vicar of Morwenstow in which English county? A. Devon B. Yorkshire C. Cornwall D. Kent Answer: C
Q5. What is considered Robert Stephen Hawker's finest poem, full of the genuine spirit of medieval adventure? A. The Quest of the Sangraal B. Idylls of the King C. The Earthly Paradise D. Tristram of Lyonesse Answer: A
Q6. Who produced the famous free translation, or paraphrase, of the Rubaiyat of Omar Khayyam? A. Sir Henry Taylor B. Robert Stephen Hawker C. Edward FitzGerald D. Martin Farquhar Tupper Answer: C
Q7. In which century did the original Persian astronomer-poet, Omar Khayyam, live? A. The ninth century B. The eleventh century C. The thirteenth century D. The fifteenth century Answer: B
Q8. Did Edward FitzGerald's translation of the Rubaiyat achieve immediate success? A. Yes, it was an instant bestseller B. No, it had little success at first, but gradually won its way C. It was completely ignored until the 20th century D. It was banned upon publication Answer: B
Q9. Who was the author of the highly didactic collection 'Proverbial Philosophy'? A. Martin Farquhar Tupper B. Edward FitzGerald C. Robert Stephen Hawker D. Sir Henry Taylor Answer: A

Q4. Which two writers are given as examples of exploded reputations from the spasmodic school? A. Dante Gabriel Rossetti and William Morris B. Sydney Dobell and Alexander Smith C. Matthew Arnold and Arthur Hugh Clough D. James Thomson and Edward FitzGerald Answer: B
Q5. James Thomson is known for which poem of pessimism and blank despair? A. Balder B. Life Drama C. The City of Dreadful Night D. The Roman Answer: C
Q6. Lord Macaulay won fame with which stirring work of poetry? A. Lost Tales of Miletus B. Lays of Ancient Rome C. King Arthur D. The Spanish Gypsy Answer: B
Q7. Which versatile author wrote the romantic epic 'King Arthur' and 'Lost Tales of Miletus'? A. Lord Macaulay B. Lord Lytton C. Thackeray D. Kingsley Answer: B
Q8. The author notes that George Eliot's poetry is considerable in quantity, but lacking in what? A. Intellectual qualities B. Moral qualities C. Real poetic inspiration D. Correct grammar Answer: C
Q9. Which of the following is a poetic work by George Eliot? A. The Legend of Jubal B. The Saint's Tragedy C. Andromeda D. Life Drama Answer: A

Q3. What was the occupation of Thomas Carlyle's father? A. A clergyman B. A stonemason C. A schoolmaster D. A wealthy landowner Answer: B

Q6. At which university did Thomas Carlyle matriculate in 1809? A. Oxford University B. Cambridge University C. Edinburgh University D. Glasgow University Answer: C
Q7. What was Carlyle's parents' original design for his career? A. That he should become a doctor B. That he should enter the Scottish Church C. That he should be a lawyer D. That he should join the military Answer: B
Q8. Why did Carlyle find it impossible to enter the Scottish Church? A. He lacked the necessary funds for training B. Radical changes in his religious views made it impossible C. He failed his university exams D. He was drafted into the army Answer: B
